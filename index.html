Creamy White Sauce Pasta

Silky penne tossed in a homemade garlic-parmesan cream sauce, finished with fresh parsley and cracked pepper. Ready in under half an hour.

Prep 10 min
Cook 15 min
Total 25 min
Serves 2
Difficulty: Easy Vegetarian Kid-friendly

0 of 12 checked

Preparation

  1. Boil salted water; cook penne al dente. Reserve ¼ cup pasta water.
  2. Melt butter; sauté garlic 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Whisk in flour 1 minute for a smooth roux.
  4. Pour in warm milk; whisk smooth. Cook 3–4 minutes.
  5. Stir in cream and parmesan; season; simmer 2 minutes.
  6. Toss pasta in sauce, loosening with pasta water.
  7. Finish with oil, parsley, parmesan; serve.

Chef's tip: Grate parmesan fresh from a block — pre-grated cheese can make the sauce grainy.
